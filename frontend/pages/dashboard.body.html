How the services work together

The Supplier Service (Zeyad) keeps the directory and decides which suppliers may receive orders. The Order Service (Ebraheem) checks eligibility with the Supplier Service and asks the Evaluation Service to convert prices to EGP. The Evaluation Service (Karim) only accepts ratings for orders that the Order Service reports as delivered, then sends the new average back to the Supplier Service.
